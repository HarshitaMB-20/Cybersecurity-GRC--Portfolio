# Banking Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Project

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates a practical Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) process for a fictional banking institution, Meridian Bank Ltd.

The objective is to assess vendor-related risks, evaluate security controls, identify gaps, maintain a risk register, and track remediation activities.

The project follows a simulated banking environment and uses fictional vendor information and assessment results.

## Business Scenario

Meridian Bank Ltd. works with multiple third-party service providers supporting critical business operations, including cloud hosting, payment processing, managed security, payroll processing and document management.

Since third parties may access sensitive information or critical systems, the bank needs a structured approach to identify, assess, monitor and manage vendor risks.

## Project Objectives

- Develop a centralized vendor inventory.
- Identify vendors requiring detailed risk assessment.
- Assess inherent and residual vendor risk.
- Evaluate key information security control areas.
- Identify control gaps and document findings.
- Maintain a risk register.
- Track remediation actions and closure evidence.
- Perform basic SQL queries for vendor and risk analysis.

## Project Components

1. **Vendor Inventory** – Centralized inventory of 10 fictional third-party vendors.
2. **Risk Assessment** – Detailed assessment of six selected vendors.
3. **Control Assessment** – 30 simulated control assessment records across five control areas.
4. **Risk Register** – Documentation of six identified vendor-related risks.
5. **Remediation Tracker** – Tracking of six remediation actions linked to identified risks.
6. **SQL Analysis** – Basic queries to filter vendors, identify assurance gaps and review risk records.

## Control Areas Assessed

- Incident Management

- Security Assurance

## Key Project Outcomes
